Liquid World Models

An engine-agnostic framework for an agent that charts a world it has not finished learning — like a hero in a dungeon whose map he hasn't found, learning it room by room. It doubts its senses and its own beliefs, testifies each night before it forgets, and remembers through priced tiers. The world model is not the game engine: the engine never doubts; the internal model is built to.

That framework is the seed of a wider programme, and this repo is now its record: the chart machinery run against real JEPA perception on the Thor, two micro-model farms whose members are called per-purpose, old games used as sealed learning fields with scores as receipts, and a spec-box league for scripted game players. Every result is filed as a full report with its process in the body — and the negatives are filed as negatives, in the index below, in the same type size as the wins.

State of the record: 2026-10-27. The demo below is the framework's own shipped output; everything after it is the filed programme, each claim verified against the document it names. Full reports: github.com/patchwork-quilt/liquid-world-model.

Three days in the uncharted dungeon (seed 7)

Same deliberately dumb explorer every day; only the chart persists between days. The maps are drawn from the model's own chart — its beliefs, its fog, its question-mark stubs for exits never taken.

Day 1 — 21 steps, goal reached; 9 exits still uncharted.

Day 2 — 27 steps, 1 surprise (a locked door, met empty-handed); uncharted exits 9 → 1.

Day 3 — 33 steps, 0 surprises; rooms not revisited have fogged over.

Read the curve honestly

Coverage and knowledge improved: uncharted exits fell 9 → 1, and the locked door surprised the model exactly once — by day 3 the chart knows it is locked and opens to the brass key. Steps did not fall (21 → 27 → 33): day 1's speed was luck (the key room came before the locked door), and the dumb policy pays honestly for beelining to a door it cannot open. The chart learned; the policy didn't. The separation is the point.

Remembering is routed, and priced

essence (4 units):  Day 1: charted 13 places (13 new), 0 surprise(s), goal reached in 21 steps. (essence carries the distilled line; the full record is in the ledger.)
truth   (12 units): {"type": "distrusted", "day": 1, "step": 5, "place": "cellar", "trust": 0.3, "reason": "senses flagged unreliable; belief not rewritten"}

One day-1 event, two routes: essence (4 units) serves the distilled line the dream left behind; truth (12 units) opens the archived episode and returns the full testified record. A third route, reflex (1 unit), serves today's still-live episode and never opens the archive at all. Retrieval is spent from the same purse as action.

Run it

python3 -m lwm.dungeon_demo --days 3 --seed 7
python3 tests/run_tests.py   # TEST SUMMARY: 10/10 PASS

Python 3, standard library only. The contract is three methods — observe(), actions(), step() — and any engine can host a liquid model; ground truth (truth()) exists for scoring only and is structurally unreachable from the agent.

Memory — structure that asks the next question

ReportVerdict
MEMORY-PATHS A 30-source study of memory structures, graded on one axis — does the walk generate the next question? FLARE's forward-looking trigger is the strongest mechanism found; Luhmann's Zettelkasten and Voyager grade 3; several popular systems score honestly low.
FALTERING-WALK Split verdict: the question-ranked walk beat the “probe nearest uncharted exit” strawman on the pre-registered metric's letter (0.0204 vs 0.0449 surprises per recall unit), but the mechanism audit shows its edge was a shrine it never re-entered — its chart was the worst of the three. The ranked question list itself is proven (the live issue ranked #1 in 6 of 6 seeds); as a truth-finder in a world this small, the walk is decoration.
FALTERING-WALK-2 The second form, built exactly as round 1 named it — a staleness floor in the disagreement term, and the morning's questions routed to the policy as intentions, not just to the feet as probes. Leg 1 passes overwhelmingly: as intentions, the questions abolish the locked door entirely — zero blocked attempts and zero door surprises in 24 arm-days, against 48–70 blocked attempts for every other arm; the brass key fetched on 24/24 mornings; 0.0017 surprises per recall unit against 0.0138 and 0.0354 — in a third of the steps. Leg 2 fails, and the pre-registered falsifier fires: final chart truth is again the worst of the arms (contents 0.972 vs 0.987). The floor verifiably promotes the shrine into the bought list — and the intention queue then starves it, because the intentions' own success shortened the day to a 7-step commute that ends at the goal before cold questions are served, while dumber arms correct the shrine by loitering. A quieter world, not a truer one. Round 1 avoided the truth by not looking; round 2 avoids it by finishing too fast to look.

The farms — small models, called per-purpose

ReportVerdict
ORIN-FARM The Orin micro-model farm as seeded: nine callable members, each under 1B parameters and each a different kind, every one earning its place with a real call on the box. (Two further tiny members were stocked later — see the harness experiment.)
ORIN-GPU The farm's brain was built without a GPU: its llama.cpp carried GGML_CUDA=OFF, so the offload flag it ignored did not exist to flip. A CUDA build in the farm's own tree, at full offload, takes llm from 37 to ~71 tok/s generation (prompt eval ~180 → ~1,118 tok/s) and the farm call to 3.59 s end-to-end — with two honesty riders kept in the headline: the registry's original CPU figure (4.7 tok/s, 29.7 s) did not reproduce on re-measurement (36.6 tok/s today; marked superseded, not explained), and partial offload is a trap on unified memory — half the layers generated at 12.5 tok/s, three times slower than pure CPU. The rule now stands in the registry: full offload or none.
ORACLE-FARM The same registry shape rebuilt on the Oracle relay's CPUs: four members as filed — five served today (llm-big joined at the scale-up, and the shim carries it since FARM-MATURITY) — outputs bit-identical to the Orin's where both exist. For micro gauges the CPU relay was not the slow tier at filing (its cold LLM call: 5.83 s against the Orin's then-29.7 s; the Orin's GPU has since cut its own call to 3.59 s — see ORIN-GPU).
ORACLE-SCALEUP llm-big (Qwen2.5-7B, hash-verified) registered on the Oracle farm: ~10× slower per generated token than the 0.6B, and the first farm digestion that returns a finished answer instead of a truncated think-trace. Also the record of the Season 0 replay below.
FARM-MATURITY The Oracle farm, finished properly. llm-big now serves through the remote shim — restarted the way it was started, all five members re-verified through the HTTP door, llm-big answering with a finished digestion in 35.6 s. And the shim's judge is settled for good: it serves the correct assembly (the jev-semantic training form on the manifest-pinned weights; the contract pair reproduces to four decimals) — and it still fails the game-safety bench, waving through 5 of 6 reckless critical-state proposals, the three-speed rejection replicated through the production door. Correct assembly, wrong domain: a general triage gauge, not a safety veto, and nothing about it is mis-wired.

Games — sealed fields, scores as receipts

ReportVerdict
GAME-SUBSTRATES Scout of game environments as learning substrate: NetHack ranked first (a fresh procedural dungeon per game, strictly turn-based so slow models can digest off-turn, death itemised as a born receipt), interactive fiction via dfrotz second (the purest language world), the house game Sail and Sink third (real-time, but its dial layer is learnable and the page self-scores).
GAME-PLAYERS Scout of existing algorithmic players as wrappable agents: qw ranked first — it exposes a declarative goals language, so an agent steers it by writing its config — then AutoAscend, then walkthrough→Jericho. The Angband Borg is sealed (in-process only): benchmark duty, wrap last.
HARNESS-EXPERIMENT Clean negative. Can barely-functioning models work inside a sophisticated harness? No: the bare 360M scored 22/40 and beat every other arm — harnessed 135M 14, the bare 0.6B control 17, harnessed 360M 6. Tiny models parrot the scaffold's intermediate format instead of executing it. The lesson the receipts argue: less scaffold, more code.
NETHACK-CHART The chart-only league: no language model anywhere — a scripted explorer feeding the framework's Chart, unmodified, inside NetHack. Median 5 true rooms × 1,061 turns across 10 runs, and the map was true: 0 merges, 0 splits, scorer-judged (one kept, unexplained one-room discrepancy in run 5).
QW-MANAGER A 0.6B farm brain managing the proven DCSS bot qw: median tied with stock, 128–128 — because only 3 of its 10 configs were valid; the failure was vocabulary precision (Runes: for the grammar's Rune:), not strategy. On the 3 seeds it actually managed, it won 2 and lost 1. Plumbing proven, management unproven.
QW-REMATCH The rematch with the 7B as manager: 10/10 configs valid — precision did arrive with size — and it bought almost nothing. The bigger manager manages by deference (stock defaults spelled longhand; 9 of 10 games event-for-event identical to stock), and its one real act of management was the worst managed game of either round. Round 1's flawed-but-bold 0.6B out-managed it.
RESIDENTS Arms 3 and 4: the Thor's own residents take the manager's chair, on the identical bench — and the posture curve's four points do not bend the way size suggests. Qwen3-Coder-30B manages exactly like the 7B — deferential, example-anchored, every game a stock replay (8/10 valid, zero combo deviations in valid configs, 10/10 games identical to control: the first arm that never lost a game, because it never changed one) — except sixty times faster (1.6 s vs 93.2 s per brief), and with one new coder-flavoured error: Rune:Lair, Rune:Orc, runes composed for branches that hold none. nemotron-3.5-lightning differs in kind: it never produced a configuration at all — given a three-line form, it spent its entire budget on hidden deliberation (answer channel empty on all 10 briefs at 512 tokens, and still empty at 4,096: 64 s, 13,757 characters of unterminated thinking, still on step 1). Residency changed the economics, not the posture: the only manager that has ever changed a game for the better remains round 1's illiterate 0.6B. The bench is now one re-runnable script, its rig spliced byte-identically from the original.
THREE-SPEED A three-speed agent in one pi harness on NetHack — TypeSafe Jev as reflex gauge, pi for decisions, pi again for slow reflection that re-sets the gates: it matches the flat scripted explorer (4,113 vs 4,563.5) and does not beat it. The layers are individually legible: reflexes alone survive 1,489 turns and chart 1 room; decisions triple the rooms; reflection provably steers (DESCEND chosen 0 times in ~300 replies before a directive, 76 times after). The chart never lied under any configuration — 0 merges / 0 splits in 11/11 runs.
